Want to build a robotics project using components you may not know yet—or do not currently have? Our vision is to help you assess how those components could work together, test the proposed system through simulation, and find relevant scientific papers and patents. This includes examining whether technologies described in your own patents, or in patents you identify, could support the integration of your system.

When a project needs capabilities beyond the platform, the proposed workflow would connect you with specialist companies offering simulation services, engineering expertise or ready-made integration plugins. The aim is to turn an initial idea into a technically supported integration plan, with clear evidence, dependencies and next steps.

Planned capabilities: automated literature and patent research, system validation and external provider connections are not yet active in this prototype. Technical relevance of a patent does not establish permission to use the protected technology.
