02 / Under the hood

Small applications.
Serious local infrastructure.

These are examples of AI functions developed and deployed around the NVIDIA DGX Spark GB10 platform, with independent web interfaces and services where applicable. They illustrate how a compact local AI environment can support speech processing, document synthesis, retrieval, scientific analysis and multimodal workflows. Individual application architectures differ; not every workload is exclusively local or runs on the GPU at all times.

Local-first intelligence

OpenAI-compatible inference endpoints and private data workflows can reduce dependency on external model APIs when configured for fully local operation.

Composable capabilities

Transcription, visual extraction, semantic organization, evidence tracking and document compilation can be reused across products.

Startup-ready questions

Every application needs a specific customer, urgent problem, measured outcome and sustainable commercial model before it becomes a business.
